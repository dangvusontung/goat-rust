# Design critique — 2026-07-22 (subagent review + follow-up discussion)

Read-only critique of DESIGN_BIBLE.md + appendices, not yet acted on except where noted.

## 1. Auto-resolve quietly starves the pantheon of the texture it needs (§2.2/§6.1 vs §8.1/§8.7 vs A.10)

Manage-by-exception means most matches get skipped. §6.1 claims skip uses "the same engine,"
which if taken literally preserves the Output number, but pundit debate (§8.7) and the Decisive
Moments axis (§8.1) need specific, nameable moments ("that finesse strike in the derby"), not an
aggregate float. Nothing in MATCH.md/CALENDAR.md says whether a skipped match retains any
beat-level event log for later narrative callback, or collapses straight to a number. If the
latter (likely, for cost), a player who skips 80%+ of a 20-year career gets a legacy score but
not a legacy case.

**Resolved 2026-07-22 (Tùng):** cadence stays season-end batch ("theo mùa") — no change to
`Intent::ApplySeasonEndLegacy` firing only at season boundaries. The remaining texture gap
this item raised (skipped matches collapsing to a number with no beat-level narrative
callback) stays open as a *content/beat-authoring* question, not an *architecture* one —
revisit under beat-library volume work, not a state-model change.

## 2. Four pantheon "schools" may just be one vector reweighted (§8.1)

Trophy-counter / eye-test romantic / stats purist / loyalty-traditionalist are described as
weighting the same 7+1 axes differently. If none of them reads a genuinely different *signal*
(only different weights on the same Output-derived numbers), an all-round great player ranks
similarly across all four — "never agree by design" risks being flavor text over one score.

## 3. Output's formula is internally inconsistent (A.10 vs A.11)

A.10 describes Output as a continuous swing (`goal_probability_swing × stage × difficulty`).
A.11 says danger lives in discrete semantic zone tiers, not a coordinate/continuous space. Taken
together, Output likely resolves to a handful of discrete values per beat — thinner than what
"the unit of measure for the legacy spine" implies.

## 4. "Sometimes no one keeps up" (emergent rival, §7.4) is underspecified

Currently reads as: absence of data + a "weak era" penalty label. Nothing commits to turning that
absence into an actual authored story beat (bespoke pundit copy for "dominant, unchallenged era"),
so as specified it risks reading as "nothing happened" rather than a deliberate narrative choice.

## 5. Formula-driven background world risks sameness across saves (§7.1 vs §2.3) — discussed in depth

Two layers, different severity:

- **Layer 1 (not actually a problem):** different seed -> different specific people/trajectories.
  Bible §2.3's "whole universe from one seed" already guarantees saves aren't literally identical.
- **Layer 2 (the real concern):** even across different seeds, background careers/transfer sagas
  are generated by the *same formula/shape* each time — variety in specific numbers, not in the
  narrative shape. Classic "FM AI league" critique; not unique to this project, it's the tradeoff
  of 20-30k full-fidelity players without hand-authoring each one.
- **Mitigating factor the critique missed:** §7.4's "lazy-promote on contact" should upgrade the
  emergent rival specifically to full-fidelity once relevant — meaning the one character who
  matters most to *your* story should already escape the generic formula. If true, "sameness"
  mostly lives in background noise the player rarely inspects closely.
- **Cheap mitigation proposed (not yet actioned):** instead of one smooth growth-curve formula,
  roll each background player into one of several career-archetype templates (early bloomer, late
  bloomer, injury-comeback arc, steady professional, mercurial talent) at genesis time, seeded.
  Variety moves into the *shape* of careers, not just their numbers — much cheaper than authoring
  20k individual stories.

**Open question for Tùng:** is #5 worth prioritizing over #1/#2 (which hit the core "manage by
exception + pantheon" promise more directly), or is it acceptable background-only cost given the
performance constraints? Not yet decided.
