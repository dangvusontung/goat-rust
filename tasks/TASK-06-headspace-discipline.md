# TASK 06 — Match depth: full headspace loop + discipline

Prereq: Phase 5 playable. Read CLAUDE.md + bible §6.2–6.3.

## Step 1 — Headspace, complete
- Full ripple loop: miss → confidence drops → timid choices surface; score → flow;
  repeated fouls against you → frustration → reckless options appear.
- Desperation as a contextual combination under pressure (late, losing, big match).
- Composure as the damper on volatility and recovery — two identical technical players
  must *feel* different in a final. Form ↔ headspace interplay per the bible: in-form
  player can have a nervy night; out-of-form player can catch fire.

## Step 2 — Discipline (bible §6.3)
- Cards emerge from systems, never a separate dice roll: beat choices (the professional
  foul / DOGSO sacrifice as a *legitimate tactical option*), frustration-driven red mist,
  the Aggression attribute, and referee personality (strict↔lenient), all through
  injected RNG.
- Referees have personality but **no per-player memory** — a dirty *reputation* tightens
  officiating instead (wire a simple dirty/clean scalar now; it becomes a Character-rep
  input in Phase 7).
- Accumulation → suspensions → missed matches → lost minutes, form, sharpness.

## Step 3 — TUI
Card/foul drama rendered in beats; bookings + suspension status visible; a discipline
identity readout on the player sheet (enforcer ↔ clean technician trend).

## Tests
Golden: seeded match where a frustration spiral produces a card via a known beat path.
Property (seeded sweeps): higher Composure → faster headspace recovery; higher
Aggression + lenient ref vs strict ref → measurable card-rate difference; suspensions
actually remove the player from selection.

## Out of scope
Reputation facets proper (Phase 7), media reaction to a red card (Phase 7/10).
